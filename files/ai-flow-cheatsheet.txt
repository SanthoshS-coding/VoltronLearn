AI ENGINEERING BASICS - BATCH 01
HOW AN AI FEATURE HANDLES A REQUEST

The short version

An AI feature is more than a model. The app around the model decides what
job to give it, what useful information to include, and what to do with
the result.

A simple request flow

1. A person asks a question or gives the app a task.
2. The app prepares the request. It can add clear instructions and relevant
   information that the model needs for this task.
3. The model returns a possible answer or other output.
4. The app can check, format, or route that output before showing it.

A small example

A visitor asks a shop website, "Can I bring my dog?"

The app finds the shop's current pet policy and includes the relevant part
with the question. The model can then draft an answer based on that context.
If the policy cannot be found, the app should not ask the model to invent it.

Keep these in mind

- Instructions describe the task. Context supplies useful details.
- Clear instructions and relevant context can help, but they do not guarantee
  a correct answer.
- A fluent answer is not proof that its facts are right.
- Try examples that are confusing or incomplete, not just the easy demo.
- Check important answers, especially when a mistake could affect a person.

Tiny experiment

Ask an AI tool the dog-policy question without providing a policy. Then giveit a short policy and ask again. Compare the two answers. Did the answerchange? Did either version make up a detail?
